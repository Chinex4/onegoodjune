## Events

- **Subtitle:** optional, shown on the event page and event cards.
- **Rich description:** new toolbar field with bold, italics, underline, links, lists, headings, large and small text (the last two and underline are available in articles too). The old plain-text field stays visible on existing events and keeps showing until the new one is filled; clear it afterwards.
- **Date range:** optional end date, shown as `Dec 12 – 14, 2026`. An event stays under Upcoming until it ends. Registration still closes when it starts.
- **Flyer:** the file was fine (1800 × 1200, sRGB). It looked dull because a 3:2 flyer was cropped to 16:9, enlarged to 1280px and compressed twice. Flyers now keep their shape, at most 1024px wide, at higher quality. Recommended upload: 2400 × 1350 px (16:9), PNG or high-quality JPG.
